Web App Development | Mental Well-Being | UX/UI Design

MindWall - Student Mental Well-Being Web App

MindWall is a student-focused mental well-being web app designed for teenagers aged 13-16. It helps students reflect on their emotions, express themselves anonymously, receive peer encouragement, and access calming activities.

Problem

Many teenagers find it difficult to express their emotions openly. MindWall responds by providing a private and supportive digital space where students can reflect, share, and feel encouraged.

Cybersecurity | Artificial Intelligence | Data Privacy

Cybersecurity & AI Industry Project - Genixa Data Breach Simulation

As part of the BuildUp Mirai Immersion programme, I investigated a simulated genomic data breach by analyzing access logs, permissions, anomalies, and incident response strategies.

Key Focus Areas

Log analysis, timeline analysis, anomaly detection, access control, least privilege, incident reporting, data privacy, and cybersecurity ethics.

Research Focus

Research Paper Goal - The Role of Cats in Transforming Human Lives

As an extension of my e-magazine, The Feline Effect, I am working toward writing and publishing a research paper on the role of cats in transforming human lives emotionally, socially, and psychologically.

Research Aim
To investigate how the human-cat bond can influence emotional well-being, reduce loneliness, and shape personal connection.
Why It Matters
This topic challenges common stereotypes about cats and explores how human-animal relationships can contribute to well-being.
