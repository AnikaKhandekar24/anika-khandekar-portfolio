Passion Project

NavaLingo AI

LinguaBridge AI: an AI-powered language learning platform for refugee and migrant students.

NavaLingo AI is a student-led social impact project designed to make language learning more accessible for displaced learners. Inspired by research into the educational challenges faced by refugee and migrant students, the project focuses on language as a pathway to education, community integration, healthcare access, employment opportunities, and reliable information.

The prototype is being developed with personalized lessons, adaptive learning pathways, conversation practice, digital literacy education, and information literacy training for real-world situations such as school communication, healthcare visits, public transportation, community integration, and everyday conversations.

Current goal: share the first prototype with NGOs, educators, researchers, and refugee-support organizations to gather feedback, explore partnerships, and make the platform more grounded in real learner needs.

MindWall contributes to Cognitive Security by supporting emotional awareness and safer decision-making for students in digital environments.
